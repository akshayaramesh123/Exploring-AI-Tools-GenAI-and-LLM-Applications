# AI Basics

## What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that enables computers and machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

## How AI Works

AI systems use data, algorithms, and models to learn patterns and make predictions or decisions.

## Examples of AI

- Chatbots
- Recommendation systems
- Voice assistants
- Image recognition
- Self-driving technology

## Applications of AI

AI is used in education, healthcare, banking, transportation, entertainment, and many other fields.

## Conclusion

Artificial Intelligence is an important technology that is changing the way people learn, work, and solve problems.
