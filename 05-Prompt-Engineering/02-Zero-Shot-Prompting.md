# Zero-Shot Prompting

## What is Zero-Shot Prompting?

Zero-shot prompting is a technique where an AI model is asked to perform a task without providing any examples in the prompt.

## How It Works

The user gives a clear instruction, and the AI uses its existing knowledge and capabilities to generate the response.

## Example

**Prompt:**

Translate the following sentence into Tamil:

Artificial Intelligence is changing the world.

No example is provided. The model performs the task directly.

## Advantages

- Simple to create
- Saves time
- Does not require examples
- Useful for straightforward tasks

## Limitations

- May not always produce the expected format
- Complex tasks may require examples or additional context
- Clear instructions are important

## Applications

- Translation
- Summarization
- Classification
- Question answering
- Text generation

## Conclusion

Zero-shot prompting is a simple and useful technique for giving direct instructions to AI models without providing examples.
