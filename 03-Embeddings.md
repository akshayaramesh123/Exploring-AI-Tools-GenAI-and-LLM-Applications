# Embeddings in LLMs

## What are Embeddings?

Embeddings are numerical representations of words, sentences, or other data. They help AI models understand the meaning and relationships between different pieces of information.

## How Embeddings Work

Text is converted into numerical vectors. Similar meanings are represented by vectors that are closer to each other in the embedding space.

## Applications

- Semantic search
- Recommendation systems
- Question answering
- Document similarity
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

## Example

The words "car" and "automobile" have similar meanings. Their embeddings can therefore be positioned close to each other in a vector space.

## Importance

Embeddings allow AI systems to compare and retrieve information based on meaning rather than only exact keyword matches.

## Conclusion

Embeddings are an important technology used in modern AI applications and Large Language Model systems.
