# History of Artificial Intelligence

## Introduction

Artificial Intelligence has developed over many decades. Researchers have continuously worked to make computers capable of solving problems and performing intelligent tasks.

## Major Milestones

### 1950 – Alan Turing
Alan Turing introduced the idea of testing whether a machine could demonstrate intelligent behavior.

### 1956 – Birth of AI
The term "Artificial Intelligence" was introduced at the Dartmouth Conference.

### 1980s – Expert Systems
Expert systems became popular for solving specific problems using rules and knowledge bases.

### 1990s – Machine Learning
Machine learning became an important approach for allowing computers to learn patterns from data.

### 2010s – Deep Learning
Deep learning achieved major improvements in areas such as image recognition, speech recognition, and natural language processing.

### 2020s – Generative AI
Generative AI became widely used for creating text, images, code, audio, and other types of content.

## Conclusion

AI has evolved from simple rule-based systems to powerful modern models capable of learning from large amounts of data and generating useful content.
