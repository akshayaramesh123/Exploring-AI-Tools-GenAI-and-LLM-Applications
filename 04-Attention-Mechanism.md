# Attention Mechanism

## What is Attention?

Attention is a mechanism used in modern neural networks to help a model focus on the most relevant parts of an input when processing information.

## Why is Attention Important?

In language tasks, different words in a sentence can have different levels of importance. Attention helps the model understand relationships between words and their context.

## Attention in Transformers

The Transformer architecture uses attention as a key component. Self-attention allows the model to consider relationships between different tokens in the input.

## Applications

- Language translation
- Text generation
- Question answering
- Text summarization
- Chatbots

## Benefits

- Helps understand context
- Handles relationships between words
- Supports processing of long sequences
- Improves language understanding

## Conclusion

Attention is one of the key concepts behind modern Large Language Models and Transformer-based AI systems.
