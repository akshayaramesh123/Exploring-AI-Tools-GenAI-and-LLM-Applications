# What is an LLM?

## Introduction

A Large Language Model (LLM) is an Artificial Intelligence model trained on large amounts of text data to understand and generate human-like language.

## How LLMs Work

LLMs process text by breaking it into smaller units called tokens. They use patterns learned during training to predict and generate suitable text.

## Examples

- GPT
- Gemini
- Claude
- Llama

## Applications

- Chatbots
- Text generation
- Translation
- Summarization
- Question answering
- Coding assistance

## Benefits

- Fast text generation
- Helps with learning and research
- Supports many language-based tasks
- Improves productivity

## Limitations

- Can generate incorrect information
- May reflect biases in training data
- Human verification is important

## Conclusion

LLMs are an important part of modern Generative AI and are widely used for language-based applications.
